## 8. How the website describes a qualified enquiry
The website describes a qualified enquiry as a prospective customer explicitly submitting an appointment, table reservation, or quote enquiry through the website with contact information.

**Filtered or NOT billable**:
- Bot submissions
- Spam
- Random clicks
- Duplicate enquiries
- Blank messages
- Ordinary visitors who do not submit an enquiry
- Unverified walk-ins

**Process details**:
- A live client portal with enquiry timestamps, phone numbers, and verification status.
- A **48-hour dispute window** for fake, unreachable, or incorrect-number leads; the site says these can be disputed and waived under its process.
- Meera should not state that every lead turns into a paying customer. She should not describe a click alone as a billable lead. Any exact eligibility rules should be confirmed in the client's written agreement.
