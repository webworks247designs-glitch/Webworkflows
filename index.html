Kind words from clients.

Questions clients ask first.

How long does a website take?

A landing page takes about a week. A business website of 4 to 8 pages takes 2 to 3 weeks, depending on how quickly we exchange content and feedback.

What do you need from me?

Your logo, text and photos if you have them. If not, I can suggest draft copy and help you find suitable images.

How many changes are included?

Two rounds of changes during design and one round after the build. Extra rounds are quoted fairly.

How does payment work?

Usually 40% to start, 30% after design approval and 30% at launch. I confirm the exact schedule in your quote before we begin.

Will my site work on phones and appear on Google?

Yes. Every site is built mobile-first, tested on real devices and set up with search-friendly markup and fast loading.

Do you help after the site is live?

Yes. You get 30 days of free fixes, then optional monthly plans for updates, edits and small improvements.

Can you work with clients in other cities or countries?

Yes. I work remotely and reply on WhatsApp and email within a few hours on working days.
